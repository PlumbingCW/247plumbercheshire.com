Mixville shows up in Cheshire's own documents as a place name. The town runs the Mixville Recreation Area at 1300 Notch Road, near the Cheshire and Prospect town line, and its plan lists Mixville Hills as open space and calls for revitalizing Mixville Park under a Mixville Master Plan. Mixville Road is a street in this part of town.

Residents plausibly use Mixville to say where they live, but no source we found draws a boundary around it. So this page describes the area by Notch Road and Mixville Road and does not claim to know exactly where Mixville starts or stops.

Homes near the Prospect line: ask which town offices apply

Because Mixville sits near the line between Cheshire and Prospect, an address can be confusing. A mailing address and a town are not always the same thing. Before you call a town office, confirm which town your house is in. Cheshire's offices handle Cheshire addresses, and our Prospect page covers the offices on the other side of the line.

The Chesprocott Health District serves both Cheshire and Prospect, so it is the office to ask about a septic system on either side. Its number is 203-272-2761. For sewer questions in Cheshire, use Town Hall at 203-271-6600, because the town plan says sewer is provided primarily in the Route 10 corridor and surrounding area. Homes outside that area may be on septic, and the right first step for a slow drain depends on which you have.

Sump pumps, floor drains and heavy rain

Any house with a basement can have a water problem after a long rain. We do not claim that Mixville homes are more likely to, because we have no source for that.

The general advice is the same everywhere. Test the sump pump before the wet season by pouring water into the pit and watching it start. Know where the floor drain leads. If a pump has failed or a drain is slow, the pages on sump pump repair and basement waterproofing explain the options and what a plumber can and cannot fix.

Plumbing permits in Cheshire come from the Building Department at 203-271-6640, under the 2022 Connecticut State Building Code, which adopts the 2021 International Plumbing Code (IPC).

Water heaters and everyday plumbing

The other calls are the everyday ones. A water heater that has stopped heating is a job for water heater repair or water heater replacement. A toilet that keeps running or a faucet that drips can wait for a regular visit, and the pages on toilet repair and faucet repair explain what is involved.
