Cheshire Village is the name used for the center of Cheshire, CT. The census treats it as a census-designated place around the intersections of Routes 10, 70 and 68, and the town's own plan describes the historic center as the area around the Routes 10, 68 and 70 intersection, with shopping, dining and civic uses.

If you live or work near the Green, South Main Street or Cornwall Avenue, this page explains what plumbing help we can talk through and which town offices to ask first.

Cheshire Village is not a separate municipality. It is the center of the town of Cheshire, so our main Cheshire page covers the same town offices, and this page adds detail that matters near the center.

Older buildings in the center, and what age does not tell us

The historic district page describes buildings dating from 1720 to 1936, in Cape, Georgian, Federal, Colonial Revival, Queen Anne and other styles. The town plan counts 41 properties along South Main Street between Main Street and Cornwall Avenue. The district mixes residential, commercial and institutional buildings.

A building's age does not tell us what is inside its walls. Pipes in an old house may have been replaced several times, or not at all. The reliable way to know is to look, and a sewer camera inspection or a plumber's check of the lines visible in the basement shows what is actually there. We do not assume any particular pipe material in any particular house.

If you are planning work that would change the outside of a building in the historic district, such as a new vent or an exterior line, ask the Cheshire Historic District Commission whether the work needs review before you schedule it. We cannot say which jobs are covered, and asking first avoids a delay.

Sewer or septic near the center of town

The town's Water Pollution Control Division serves about 5,000 properties through roughly 120 miles of sewer piping and ten pump stations. The town plan says sewer service is provided primarily in the Route 10 corridor and the area around it, which includes the center of town. Streets a little farther out may be on septic instead.

This page cannot tell you which side of that line your street is on. If you are unsure, ask the Water Pollution Control Division through Town Hall at 203-271-6600. A homeowner with a septic system can also ask the Chesprocott Health District at 203-272-2761. When a main drain runs slowly, drain cleaning is the usual first step, and a clog that keeps coming back is a good reason to ask for a camera look.

Storms, Cornwall Avenue and wet basements

The town plan records that Cornwall Avenue, Routes 68 and 70, near the canal crossing, floods during large storms and is sometimes closed.

That is a finding about a road, not about any home. If you find water in a basement after heavy rain, the plumbing side is usually a sump pump, a floor drain or a failed line, and our pages on sump pump repair and basement waterproofing explain those options. If the water is coming through the foundation from outside, a plumber may not be the right first call, and we will say so.

Before you call about a plumbing problem

These steps apply in almost any house or shop and are safe for most people to do.

Find the main shutoff

Know where the main water valve is and turn it fully clockwise if water is running where it should not.

Stop using the fixture

If a drain is backing up, stop running water in the building until it is cleared.

Look before you call

Note which fixtures are affected and whether water is clear or dirty. That helps us ask the right questions.

Call and ask

Call (203) 720-6269 and describe the problem and the address. Ask about timing and what the visit will include.

Offices to ask in Cheshire

The Cheshire Building Department, 84 South Main Street, 203-271-6640, issues plumbing permits through the Building Official. Connecticut plumbing work falls under the 2022 State Building Code, which adopts the 2021 International Plumbing Code (IPC). Permit rules vary by project, so ask before a larger job such as a new water heater or a rerouted drain. For sewer questions, use Town Hall at 203-271-6600.

Source: Town of Cheshire Building Department and Water Pollution Control Division pages.
