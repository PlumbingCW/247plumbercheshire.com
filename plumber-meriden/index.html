Winter first: Meriden in the cold months

Cold snaps are when the most urgent calls arrive. A pipe in an unheated back hall or a building with poor insulation can freeze and split overnight. Shut off the main, open a low faucet and call. Our burst pipe repair service runs around the clock, and a no-heat boiler is a boiler repair call.

Multi-family buildings add a twist. The shutoff you need may be shared, and a tenant may not know where it is. If you manage a building, label the main and the unit shutoffs before winter.

Meriden's water and sewer divisions

The City of Meriden's Water Division, at 117 Parker Avenue, serves the city's customers with water from reservoir and well sources, and the city also runs a Water Pollution Control Division. A break in the public main or a municipal sewer backup is theirs. A failure inside your building or in your private line is a plumbing call.

We do not state a hardness figure for Meriden, because the division's page does not give one. Test your tap or ask the Water Division for its report. If a drain keeps backing up, a sewer camera inspection shows whether the private lateral is the cause.

Hubbard Park and older buildings

Meriden's long manufacturing history left older commercial blocks and tightly packed neighborhoods. Hubbard Park is the city's best-known landmark, and the surrounding streets have homes of many ages. Older buildings can still carry original cast iron or clay drains, and a camera look before a repair saves guessing.

We serve ZIP codes 06450 and 06451, covering both sides of the city. Commercial customers downtown and along the corridors use our commercial plumbing team after hours. Multi-family owners often ask about repiping when galvanized lines keep failing.

Rental buildings and landlords

Meriden has many rental units, and a plumbing emergency in one of them involves a tenant, a landlord and sometimes an insurer. If you manage a building, give tenants a number to call and tell them where the shutoffs are. If you rent, report a leak right away and photograph the damage before anything is moved.

We can work with a property manager on access and scheduling, and we give a clear price before work starts so approvals go quickly.
