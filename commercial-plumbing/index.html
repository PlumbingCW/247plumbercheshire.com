Emergency commercial plumbing in Cheshire, CT is a call to place the moment a leak, backup or burst pipe threatens your doors. Our 24/7 line is open after hours, on weekends and on holidays. Shut off water at the affected fixture or the main, keep customers away from wet floors, and call.

Commercial plumbing covers the pipes, drains, fixtures and water heating of shops, offices, restaurants, medical suites and small industrial spaces. The loads are bigger than a home's, the rules for a business can differ, and downtime costs money by the hour.

After-hours emergencies for a business

A restroom overflow or a kitchen backup after closing can wreck floors by morning. If the problem is a single fixture, close its supply valve and stop use. If water is spreading, shut the main and call. Photograph the damage for your insurer and note the time.

Tell dispatch the type of business and whether food service or customers are affected. A restaurant with a failed grease line or a medical office with a lost water supply needs a different response from a quiet office with a dripping tap.

Common commercial plumbing problems

Floor drain backup in a kitchen

Grease, food solids and starch build up. Regular jetting prevents the Saturday-night version.

Restroom clogs and slow drains

High-traffic restrooms see paper products and foreign objects that residential lines do not.

Water heater failure

A commercial tank or tankless system feeds sinks, dishwashers and cleaning, and its failure can close a kitchen.

Leaks in a ceiling or shared wall

Water spreads between tenants. Fast detection limits the claims.

Low pressure on upper floors or in sinks

Valves, regulators or corroded lines are likely suspects.

Grease, backflow and the town's rules

Food-service businesses in Cheshire operate under the town's fats, oils and grease program, administered through the Water Pollution Control Division, which publishes a FOG ordinance and application form. We can service the drains and lines your kitchen depends on, and we do not decide what the town requires of you, so ask the Water Pollution Control Division about your permit and cleaning schedule.

Backflow prevention devices protect the public water supply from contamination, and many commercial properties have them. Testing is a regulated task and the requirements come from the water utility. Ask the Regional Water Authority what applies to your property.

commercial plumbing and the plumbing code

Plumbers in Connecticut work to the 2021 IPC through the 2022 State Building Code. Commercial work can add rules on top of the plumbing code, so the Building Department sets the path for each job. The town Building Official, reachable at 203-271-6640, is the final word on what a Cheshire permit requires.
