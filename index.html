Local to Cheshire

Emergency plumbing across Cheshire, from Cheshire Village to Highland Avenue

The 24/7 line covers the whole town. That includes Cheshire Village, the Cheshire Historic District, Moss Farms Village along West Johnson and Highland Avenue and the northeast side toward Southington and Meriden.

Public water in town comes from the Regional Water Authority, and a good share of homes use private septic instead of the town sewer, which the Sewer Division says serves about 5,000 properties. Tell dispatch which system your house uses, since it changes the first steps for a backup.

That mix matters in an emergency. A backup in a sewered house and a slow drain in a septic house call for different first steps, so tell dispatch which you have. We also cover ZIP codes 06410, 06408 and 06411 in Cheshire, and the nearby towns of Southington, Wallingford, Prospect and Meriden, with ZIPs 06489, 06492, 06712 and 06450.

Cold snaps put the most pressure on Connecticut plumbing. An exterior wall, an unheated garage or a crawlspace can freeze a line overnight, and a burst pipe repair call at 3 a.m. is a familiar one. In wet seasons the calls shift toward basements, where a working sump pump matters, and sump pump repair is the quick fix.

If you want to look around before you need us, start with the main shutoff. Know where it is, test it, and keep our number handy. The best emergency plumber is the one you reach in the first five minutes.
