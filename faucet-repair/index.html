Emergency faucet repair in Cheshire, CT begins under the sink. Turn the small shutoff valves clockwise to stop water to the faucet. If a valve will not turn, shut the main and call our 24/7 line. A supply line that has burst or a faucet that will not shut off can flood a kitchen quickly.

Faucet repair covers the faucet body and cartridge, the supply lines and the angle stops under the sink. Many faucets can be rebuilt with a new cartridge or seals, and some are better replaced.

When a faucet problem is urgent

A faucet that will not shut off, a supply line that is spraying and a shutoff valve that drips or will not close are the urgent cases. Also urgent is any leak under a sink in a cabinet, since the wood and drywall soak up water.

Place a bucket under a drip, shut off the stop valve if it works, and keep valuables out of the cabinet. If a valve is seized or corroded, do not force it with pliers, because a broken valve stem releases the full line pressure.

Why faucets leak

Most faucet leaks come from a few wear parts.

Drip from the spout

A worn cartridge, washer or seat. Replacement is usually quick.

Leak at the handle base

Worn O-rings or a loose bonnet.

Water under the sink

A worn supply line, a leaking stop valve or a failed drain connection.

Low flow from one faucet

A clogged aerator or cartridge with scale.

Handle stiff or loose

Mineral buildup, worn parts or a stripped screw.

Mineral buildup and Cheshire water

Cheshire water comes from the Regional Water Authority, and its report does not state a hardness figure for the town. So we do not quote one. If aerators and showerheads clog quickly in your house, test your tap or ask the utility for its water quality report. Scale builds in any fixture where heated water evaporates or passes slowly.

A vinegar soak loosens scale on an aerator in about an hour, which is a simple home fix for low flow. If the cartridge is scaled, replacement is usually cleaner than cleaning.

Repair or replace the faucet

A plumber quotes both options when it makes sense.

  • ✓

    Repair when the faucet is in good condition and parts are available for the model.

  • ✓

    Replace if the finish is peeling, parts are discontinued or repairs keep recurring.

  • ✓

    Replace the shutoff valves at the same time if they are old and weeping, since a valve that fails later floods the cabinet.

  • ✓

    Replace flexible supply lines when they are older, since the braided lines can fail from age.

Code and permits for faucet repair

Connecticut plumbing work falls under the 2022 State Building Code, which adopts the 2021 International Plumbing Code (IPC). Replacing a cartridge or a faucet is routine, while adding new supply lines or fixtures can fall under permit rules. Permit questions go to the Cheshire Building Department at 203-271-6640, since the Building Official issues plumbing permits in town.
