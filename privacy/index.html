Who we are

Cheshire 24/7 Plumbing operates this website at 247plumbercheshire.com. Our email is contact@247plumbercheshire.com. We serve Cheshire, CT and nearby towns by phone.

What we collect

We are a call-only service, and this site has no forms. When you call, we may collect your name, phone number, address and a description of the plumbing problem so that we can schedule service. Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes.

Like most websites, our hosting provider may keep standard server logs, such as IP address and pages visited. We use this information to keep the site running and secure.

How we use it

We use call information to schedule and perform plumbing service, to follow up on a visit and to improve our service. We do not sell your personal information.

Sharing

We may share the details of a job with the plumber assigned to it, and with service providers who help us operate. We may disclose information when the law requires it.

Your choices

You can ask us what information we hold about you, or ask us to delete it, by calling or emailing us. If you do not want a call recorded, tell the dispatcher at the start of the call.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version is always on this page.

Questions?

Call or email us any time.
