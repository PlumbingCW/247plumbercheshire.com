Our 24/7 line is (203) 720-6269. Call for burst pipes, backups, no hot water and any plumbing problem that cannot wait. Tell dispatch what is happening, your address and whether you are on public sewer or septic, and we will book the earliest appointment available.

Permit questions for your job

If you are planning work that may need a permit, the Cheshire Building Department at 84 South Main Street handles plumbing permits under the 2022 Connecticut State Building Code, which adopts the 2021 International Plumbing Code (IPC).
