Emergency garbage disposal repair in Cheshire, CT begins with the switch. Turn the disposal off at the wall, and if you plan to look inside, switch off its breaker too. Never reach into a disposal with your hand. Our 24/7 line is open if the unit is leaking or the sink is backing up.

Garbage disposal repair covers jams, motors that hum or will not start, leaks at the mounting, the drain connection or the housing, and clogs below the unit. A disposal that is leaking from the body is replaced, and one that is jammed usually can be cleared.

Jammed or humming: what to do first

A humming disposal with no spinning is jammed. Turn it off and unplug it or flip the breaker. Use an Allen wrench in the bottom of many disposals to turn the shaft back and forth, or use a wooden spoon handle from above to free the blades. Press the red reset button on the bottom after the jam clears.

If the unit trips the reset again or leaks from the bottom, stop. A motor that fails repeatedly or a housing that leaks cannot be repaired economically, and a replacement is the safe choice.

Common disposal problems

Each symptom points to a different part.

Humming, no spin

Jammed flywheel. Free it with the wrench and reset.

Silent and no hum

Reset tripped, outlet or switch failed, or the motor is dead.

Water at the base of the unit

A cracked housing or failed seal. Replace the disposal.

Leak at the sink flange

The mounting gasket or putty failed. Usually fixable.

Sink drains slowly or backs up

A clog downstream of the disposal, not the unit itself.

What should not go down a disposal

Grease, coffee grounds in quantity, fibrous vegetables, potato peels and pasta rice swell or coat the drain and cause stoppages. Bones, fruit pits and hard shells jam the blades. Use cold running water during and after use so fats move along rather than coat the pipe.

This matters most for Cheshire homes on septic. A septic tank handles food solids poorly, and heavy disposal use fills the tank faster. The Chesprocott Health District serves Cheshire, Prospect and Wolcott and can advise on septic maintenance. If a home on septic uses a disposal heavily, pump the tank more often.

Repair or replace the disposal

The unit's age and the problem decide it.

Repair when

  • The unit is jammed but otherwise sound
  • The gasket or flange is leaking
  • A switch or outlet has failed

Replace when

  • The body is leaking
  • The motor trips repeatedly
  • It is more than about a decade old and failing
