Wells and septic: how the system changes the repair

Some Prospect properties rely on private wells and septic systems rather than public water and sewer. The Chesprocott Health District, which serves Cheshire, Prospect and Wolcott, lists private wells and well water treatment among its resources, and it is the local office for well and septic questions. We handle the plumbing in the house, and the health district handles the regulation.

A well changes diagnosis. Low pressure may point to the pressure tank or pump, not the house pipes. A pump that cycles rapidly can mean a leak. Water that comes out cloudy after a storm is a testing question, not a plumbing one. Tell us what you notice, and we will say whether it is a plumbing issue or one for the well contractor or health district.

Frozen lines and cold nights in hill country

Prospect's open, wooded lots can see hard cold, and a line that runs through an unheated garage or crawlspace is the usual victim. If a pipe freezes, open the faucet, warm the area gently and call. Our burst pipe repair team handles splits, and we advise insulating exposed runs before the first deep freeze.

Wet basements are the other recurring call. Heavy rain and snowmelt push groundwater toward foundations on sloped lots, and a sump pump is the front line. If yours has failed, sump pump repair is often a quick job once the cause is found. For a longer-term fix, basement waterproofing addresses where the water enters.

Pumps, pressure tanks and what to check

If you are on a well, learn the basics of the system. The pressure tank holds water under pressure so the pump does not run constantly. A tank that has lost its air charge makes the pump cycle fast, and a pump that cycles fast wears out. A pressure gauge near the tank shows the range your system holds.

When pressure drops, check the breaker for the well pump and look for a leak in the house before suspecting the well. If the pump runs and no water arrives, that is a well contractor call. For anything inside the house, we handle it.
