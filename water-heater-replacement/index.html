Emergency water heater replacement in Cheshire, CT usually starts with water on a basement floor. Close the cold water valve above the tank and shut off the breaker or gas control, then call our 24/7 line. We can talk you through the shutoff and book the earliest appointment we have.

Water heater replacement means removing the failed tank and installing a new one with fresh connections, a relief valve, and a proper vent or drain. Replacing, rather than repairing, is the right call when the tank itself has failed or when repairs keep stacking up on an old unit.

A failed tank in the basement: what to do first

Start with the water supply. The cold inlet valve is on the pipe entering the top of the heater. Turn it fully clockwise. Then kill power or gas, and open a hot tap upstairs to relieve pressure.

Move anything valuable out of the wet area and use a wet vacuum if the amount of water is small. A tank can release a lot of water, which is why a heater in a finished space near a floor drain is the lucky case. If water is reaching electrical outlets or the panel, stay out and call us from a dry spot.

Fast swap or planned upgrade

Most emergency replacements stay close to the existing setup so the home has hot water quickly. A planned upgrade lets you consider other options.
