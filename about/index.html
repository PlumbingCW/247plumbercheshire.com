What we do

Cheshire 24/7 Plumbing is a plumbing dispatch line serving Cheshire, CT and nearby Southington, Wallingford, Prospect and Meriden. When you call, a person answers, asks what is happening and books the earliest appointment available with a plumber.

We cover emergencies such as burst pipes, sewer backups and failed water heaters, and planned work such as installs, repiping and sump pump replacements.

How a call works

The process is meant to be simple and the same every time.

  • 1

    You call. Dispatch answers at any hour, including nights, weekends and holidays.

  • 2

    We ask a few questions. What is happening, where in the house, whether water is spreading and whether the main is off.

  • 3

    We guide you. If water or a drain is the problem, we explain how to limit damage while the visit is set.

  • 4

    A plumber visits. The plumber diagnoses the problem and gives a clear price before work starts.

  • 5

    You decide. Work begins when you agree to the price.

How we work

Every job starts with a clear price before work begins. We explain what we find in plain language, and we say so when a cheaper repair is the better choice. We do not promise arrival times we cannot keep, and we do not take work we are not equipped to do.

Our service is call-only. There are no forms, because a plumbing problem is easier to solve in a conversation. Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes.

What we know about Cheshire

Public water in Cheshire is supplied by the Regional Water Authority. The town's Sewer Division reports serving about 5,000 properties, so a large share of homes use septic systems, and the Chesprocott Health District, which serves Cheshire, Prospect and Wolcott, handles well and septic questions. Plumbing permits go through the Building Department at 84 South Main Street, 203-271-6640.

That local picture shapes what we ask on a call. A backup in a sewered home and a slow drain in a septic home need different first steps, and a well home has a pump and pressure tank that a public-water home does not. We ask early so the plumber arrives prepared.

The work we dispatch for

Most calls fall into a few groups. Water problems include burst pipes, hidden leaks and water service line failures. Drain problems include clogged drains, main line backups and sewer line repair. Heating and hot water include water heater repair, water heater replacement and boiler repair.

We also handle commercial plumbing for shops, offices and kitchens, and planned jobs such as repiping and plumbing installation. If your problem is not on the list, call anyway. We will tell you honestly whether we can help.

Check any plumber's license

In Connecticut, you can verify a plumber's license through the Department of Consumer Protection's license lookup. We encourage you to do that with any contractor you hire. Ask the plumber who arrives for their license and confirm it before work starts.

Permit rules vary by project, so ask the Cheshire Building Department before a larger job. A licensed plumber can tell you what we expect applies, and the department decides.

What we will not do

We will not promise a response time, and we will not quote a price over the phone for a job we have not seen. We will not push a replacement when a repair will do, and we will not tell you a problem is an emergency when it can wait for a regular visit.

If you smell gas, we ask you to leave the building and call 911 or Yankee Gas at 877-944-5325 before you call us. Gas leaks are a utility and fire department matter first.

How we treat codes and permits

Plumbers in Connecticut work to the 2021 International Plumbing Code (IPC) as adopted in the 2022 Connecticut State Building Code. In Cheshire, the Building Department at 203-271-6640 issues plumbing permits, and we send you there when a job needs one.

We do not publish licence numbers, staff names or review counts on this site, because none of them can be checked from a page like this. What you can check is the phone line and the official town offices named on these pages, and we are glad for you to do that before you book.
